## Contents of the Appendix

1. **Quality of Earnings (QoE) & Normalized EBITDA Bridge** — Line-by-line reconciliation from reported continuing IFRS EBITDA (€34.2M) to pro forma normalized EBITDA (€43.5M) and post-cost-synergy EBITDA (€52.0M).
2. **Synergy Build & After-Tax NPV** — Phasing, one-off implementation costs (€10.0M), and net present value (€59.0M) of actionable cost synergies; revenue synergies treated strictly as upside.
3. **WACC Derivation & 5-Year Standalone DCF** — CAPM build (9.0% WACC), unlevered free cash flow projections (2027E–2031E), terminal value derivation, and sensitivity analysis.
4. **Trading Comparables & Precedent Transactions** — Peer multiple benchmarking (7.3x–8.8x IQR) and European industrial motion control precedent deals (8.5x–10.0x applied range).
